CRYPTO TRACE

Lost cryptocurrency: what a blockchain investigation can reveal

When funds leave a wallet after a scam, a transaction hash can help analysts map transfers, identify interacting addresses and document movements to known services. Tracing can generate leads, but it cannot by itself reverse a transaction or guarantee recovery.

ASSET RECOVERY

What to do in the first 24 hours after a crypto scam

Save transaction IDs, wallet addresses, chat logs, emails, exchange receipts and a clear timeline. Contact the exchange or payment provider through its official channels and report the incident to the appropriate authorities. Never share seed phrases or private keys with a recovery service.

GREY-HAT EXPLAINED

Grey-hat hacking: the line between discovery and unauthorized access

Grey-hat activity can involve finding weaknesses without prior permission. Good intentions do not automatically make access lawful. Zenith’s educational coverage focuses on defensive validation, written authorization, responsible disclosure and remediation—not breaking into systems or extracting data.

RESPONSIBLE DISCLOSURE

How ethical vulnerability reporting protects organizations

Define the scope first, test only systems you are authorized to assess, minimize data access, preserve a careful record and report findings privately with clear reproduction steps. Stop immediately if testing exposes sensitive information or risks service disruption.

OSINT

Using open-source intelligence to support a digital asset case

Public records, domain history, impersonation clues and openly available wallet labels can provide context for a case. Each lead should be independently verified, dated and distinguished from assumptions before it is shared with investigators or counsel.

FRAUD PREVENTION

Recovery scams: avoid losing money a second time

Beware of guaranteed recovery promises, unsolicited direct messages, fake law-enforcement identities and demands for advance “taxes” or release fees. Legitimate professionals should explain their process, limits, costs and credentials in writing.

Read full article →
EXCHANGE SUPPORT

When traced crypto reaches an exchange

Document the transaction path and submit a concise evidence package to the relevant exchange’s official compliance or law-enforcement channel. A service may be able to review or preserve information under its policies and applicable law, but freezing or returning assets is never assured.

Read full article →
INCIDENT RESPONSE

After a wallet compromise: secure what remains

Use a clean device, revoke suspicious sessions and approvals where appropriate, move remaining assets to a newly secured wallet, and contact affected providers. Do not reuse a compromised seed phrase or install tools sent by strangers claiming they can recover funds.

How a structured case file helps asset recovery efforts

A useful report brings together a timeline, transaction hashes, wallet addresses, amounts and asset types, platform case numbers, communications and copies of reports filed. Keep original records intact and label conclusions by confidence level.

RESEARCH & ANALYSIS

From signals to evidence.

Digital investigations can involve blockchain activity, public records, online identities, financial movements and technical indicators. The goal is not more information—it is better verified information. Our support may include transaction-path analysis, evidence organization, open-source research and guidance on reporting to exchanges, banks, legal advisers or law enforcement. Blockchain tracing identifies observable movement; it does not guarantee that assets can be frozen or recovered.
