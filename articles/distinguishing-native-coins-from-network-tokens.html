Distinguishing Native Coins From Network Tokens

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Native coins pay network fees on their chain, while tokens are represented through network-specific mechanisms such as smart contracts.

Practical steps

Document network, asset type, contract where applicable, quantity and fee asset separately.

Limitations and cautions

Similar names and logos may refer to unrelated assets; verify chain and contract.

Responsible checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
