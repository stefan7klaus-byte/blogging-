Preserving Evidence When a Scam Website Disappears

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A disappearing website may remove context, so preserve records already available promptly and safely.

Practical steps

Save the full URL, dated screenshots, emails, transaction hashes, agreements and receipts; avoid unsafe links.

Limitations and cautions

Screenshots preserve one view but do not prove every claim made on the site.

Responsible checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
