Preserving Exchange Emails and Account Alerts

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Gather the key details

Keep exchange emails, security alerts and support messages in their original format where possible, with sender details, timestamps and relevant headers if available.

2. Preserve and organize records

Record the mailbox, collection date and related account or ticket number. Export relevant messages securely and maintain a separate note explaining their relevance.

3. Review before sharing

Sender names can be spoofed. Verify requests by opening the exchange's official app or site independently, and never reply with passwords or wallet secrets.

Practical checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and relevant records.
  • Separate observable facts from assumptions.
  • Protect account credentials and personal information.
  • Use official channels and independently verify providers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
