How to Preserve Email Headers in a Fraud Case

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Why this matters

Email headers can contain routing and authentication details that are lost when only the visible message text is copied.

Practical steps

Use the email provider's original-message or download option, preserve the file securely and note the mailbox and capture date.

Limitations and care

Header interpretation can be technical; do not publish private addresses or assume a display name verifies a sender.

Checklist

  • Keep originals unchanged and store them securely.
  • Record source, date and time zone.
  • Separate confirmed facts from estimates.
  • Share only relevant information through verified channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
