Planning Your Next Steps After a Crypto Scam

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Take the practical first steps

Start by protecting accounts and stopping further transfers to the suspected scammer. Contact the relevant exchange, bank or payment provider through verified official channels and ask what incident-reporting steps are available.

2. Preserve and verify

Organize transaction IDs, addresses, receipts, messages, URLs and a dated timeline. Keep report reference numbers and note what has been submitted, what remains outstanding and which claims are still uncertain.

3. Protect yourself from further harm

Avoid urgent decisions driven by promises of guaranteed recovery. Depending on the situation, official authorities, a qualified lawyer or a platform's fraud team may be appropriate. Tracing documents activity but cannot guarantee a refund.

Practical checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information or engaging a provider.
  • Document reports and follow-up reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
