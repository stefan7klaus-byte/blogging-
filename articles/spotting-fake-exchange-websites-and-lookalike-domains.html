Scam investigation and fraud evidence

Spotting Fake Exchange Websites and Lookalike Domains

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Understand the warning signs

Fraudulent exchange pages imitate branding or use similar spellings. HTTPS alone does not prove a business is genuine.

Preserve relevant records

Record the full domain, how you reached it, company details, terms, contact methods and payment requests; verify independently.

Take safer next steps

Do not upload identity documents or deposit funds until the service is independently verified.

Checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and original records.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Protect personal information and account credentials.
  • Use official reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
