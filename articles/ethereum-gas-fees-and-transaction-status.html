Blockchain networks and tracing limitations

Ethereum Gas Fees and Transaction Status

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Understand the data

Ethereum transactions may show gas-related fields and execution status. A transaction can be included in a block yet fail during execution, and a fee may still be charged.

2. Record the relevant details

Record transaction hash, status, block, gas or fee fields as displayed, and explorer URL. Keep token events separate from top-level native-asset value where appropriate.

3. Avoid unsupported conclusions

Do not equate 'included' with 'successful' or assume a failed transaction had no cost. Interpret fields in the network context.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm the network and exact transaction identifier.
  • Save source URLs and dates checked.
  • Separate direct observations from analysis.
  • Record uncertainty and alternative explanations.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
