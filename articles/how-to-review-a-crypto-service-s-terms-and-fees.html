How to Review a Crypto Service's Terms and Fees

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Take practical first steps

Read the service's terms for fees, withdrawal conditions, custody arrangements, supported networks, complaint channels and data handling. Check that the terms belong to the exact legal entity and domain.

2. Verify and preserve

Save a copy or note the date of the terms reviewed, and compare advertised claims with the written agreement. Seek independent advice if a material term is unclear.

3. Protect yourself from further harm

Terms can change and a polished interface does not establish legitimacy. Do not accept pressure to pay extra charges that were not clearly disclosed or independently verified.

Practical checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information or engaging a provider.
  • Document reports and follow-up reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
