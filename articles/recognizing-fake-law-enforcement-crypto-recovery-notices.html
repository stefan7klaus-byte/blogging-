Recognizing Fake Law-Enforcement Crypto Recovery Notices

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Scammers sometimes imitate agencies and claim that a fee is required to release recovered assets.

Recommended lawful workflow

Verify the agency using independently located official contact information and report suspicious messages.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not pay a wallet address or trust a phone number supplied only in the suspicious message.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and transaction hashes.
  • Record the network, amount, timestamp and time zone.
  • Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
  • Use official platform, legal and law-enforcement channels.
  • Never share wallet seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
