Evidence collection and reporting

Exchange Records to Request Through Official Channels

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Build a clear record

Ask the exchange through its official support, compliance or law-enforcement reporting process which records it can provide and what legal process may be required. Include relevant transaction hashes, timestamps, account identifiers you legitimately possess and the incident reference.

2. Preserve the source material

Keep a copy of the request, the submission date, case number and any response. Share only information relevant to the incident and use the provider's secure submission method rather than sending account details to an unverified address.

3. Review for accuracy and limits

An exchange may restrict disclosures or require formal legal process. Do not impersonate law enforcement, attempt to access another person's account, or assume the platform can freeze or return assets just because a transaction appears in its system.

Practical checklist

  • Use consistent dates and time zones.
  • Keep original records unchanged and secure.
  • Link factual statements to supporting records.
  • Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
