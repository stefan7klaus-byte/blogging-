Keeping a Chain of Custody for Digital Evidence

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Why this matters

A simple chain-of-custody record shows who collected, copied, transferred or accessed an item and when.

Practical steps

Assign an evidence ID, preserve originals, record transfers and access, and use secure storage with appropriate permissions.

Limitations and care

A log supports transparency but cannot by itself prove an item's authenticity; follow applicable rules.

Checklist

  • Keep originals unchanged and store them securely.
  • Record source, date and time zone.
  • Separate confirmed facts from estimates.
  • Share only relevant information through verified channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
