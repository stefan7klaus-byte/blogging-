Why Wallet Clustering Is Not Proof of Identity

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Understand the data

Wallet-clustering methods group addresses using heuristics or other evidence, but clusters may contain errors or combine activity for operational reasons. Labels can be outdated or copied between services.

2. Record the relevant details

When referencing a cluster, record the provider or method, date checked, cited rationale and independent corroboration. Distinguish a third-party label from direct ledger observation.

3. Avoid unsupported conclusions

A cluster label is not proof of a person's identity or control. Avoid naming individuals based solely on heuristics; document uncertainty and alternatives.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm the network and exact transaction identifier.
  • Save source URLs and dates checked.
  • Separate direct observations from analysis.
  • Record uncertainty and alternative explanations.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
