What to Prepare Before Contacting Zenith Hackers Intelligence

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A short, organized summary helps you explain what happened without sharing unnecessary sensitive information.

Recommended workflow

Prepare dates, networks, asset names, transaction hashes, platform case references, actions already taken and your specific questions.

Limitations and safeguards

Use independently verified contact channels and do not expect any service to guarantee recovery.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
