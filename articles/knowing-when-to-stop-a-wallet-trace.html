Knowing When to Stop a Wallet Trace

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A trace should stop when the evidence no longer supports a reliable next step or the scope is exhausted.

Recommended workflow

Document the last verified event, unresolved questions, unavailable records and what additional information might help.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not fill gaps with guesses or imply that every path can be followed to an individual.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record the network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
