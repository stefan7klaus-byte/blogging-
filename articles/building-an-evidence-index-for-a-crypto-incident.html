Evidence collection and reporting

Building an Evidence Index for a Crypto Incident

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Why this matters

An evidence index gives each record a stable reference and describes its contents so a reviewer can locate it quickly.

Practical steps

Assign a unique label, filename, date, source, description and related event; note any redacted copy.

Limitations and care

An index is not the evidence itself; do not alter originals to fit a narrative.

Checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and original records.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Protect personal information and account credentials.
  • Use official reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
