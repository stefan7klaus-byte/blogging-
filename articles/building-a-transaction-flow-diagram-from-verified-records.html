Building a Transaction Flow Diagram From Verified Records

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A transaction-flow diagram can summarize known movements when every edge is tied to a verifiable record.

Recommended workflow

Use nodes for addresses or services and arrows for transfers; label each edge with hash, chain, amount, asset and time, and mark uncertain links.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not use diagram proximity or arrow direction to imply identity or guilt.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
