Case Study: Reconstructing a Scam Timeline From Mixed Records

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A fictional case may combine chat messages, exchange withdrawals and public blockchain transactions.

Recommended workflow

Build a timeline that labels each event by source and time zone, then link on-chain events by exact hashes where possible.

Limitations and safeguards

Timing and sequence help organize evidence but do not independently prove motive or identity.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record the network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
