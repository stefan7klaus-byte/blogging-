Redacting a Case Report Before Sharing It

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A public or broadly shared report may expose account identifiers, contact details or unrelated third-party information.

Recommended workflow

Make a copy, redact only what is unnecessary, inspect the exported file for hidden layers or metadata and keep an unredacted original securely.

Limitations and safeguards

Redaction should not alter the evidentiary original or obscure facts needed by an authorized reviewer.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
