Blockchain networks and tracing limitations

Why an Exchange Deposit Address May Be Shared

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Custodial services may use shared addresses, memo fields or internal accounting to distinguish customer deposits.

Practical steps

Record the address, network, memo or tag, exchange reference and official deposit instructions.

Limitations and cautions

An address may identify a service, not an individual customer.

Responsible checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
