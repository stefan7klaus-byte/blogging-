Fake Exchanges and Withdrawal-Fee Traps

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Identify and record the warning signs

Be cautious when a platform advertises easy profits but blocks withdrawals until a user pays extra fees, taxes or verification deposits. Independently check the domain and business details before using a service.

2. Preserve supporting records

Keep screenshots of balances and error messages, terms shown at the time, transaction references, support tickets and payment instructions. A displayed balance on a website is not proof that assets exist in a withdrawable wallet.

3. Choose safer next steps

Do not pay additional release fees. Contact the payment provider or exchange that processed the transfer using its official website, secure the accounts involved and report the platform to relevant authorities.

Practical checklist

  • Save original messages, URLs, receipts and transaction IDs.
  • Record dates, amounts and platform reference numbers.
  • Keep original files unchanged and store copies securely.
  • Use verified official channels to report the incident.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
