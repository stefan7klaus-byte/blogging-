Setting Realistic Expectations for Blockchain Tracing

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Public blockchain data may document asset movements but may not identify a person or reverse a transfer.

Practical steps

Define the question, available records, deliverables, uncertainty and limits before starting any review.

Limitations and cautions

No tracing or recovery provider can honestly guarantee every outcome.

Responsible checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
