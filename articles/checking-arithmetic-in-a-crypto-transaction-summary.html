Checking Arithmetic in a Crypto Transaction Summary

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Simple calculation mistakes can make a tracing report confusing or misleading.

Recommended workflow

Recalculate transfer amounts and fees separately, state units and decimal precision, and document any fiat conversion rate and timestamp.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not treat gross transaction volume as a victim's net loss.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record the network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
