Creating a Clear Timeline for a Zenith Enquiry

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A concise timeline helps a service provider understand events without receiving an unstructured archive.

Recommended workflow

List date/time with zone, event, source, transaction hash or case reference and action taken; mark uncertain dates clearly.

Limitations and safeguards

Share only relevant information through independently verified channels.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
