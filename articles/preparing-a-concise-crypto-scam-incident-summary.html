Preparing a Concise Crypto Scam Incident Summary

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A short incident summary can help an exchange, support team or reporting body understand the issue quickly.

Recommended workflow

Include what happened, when it happened, amounts and assets, transaction hashes, relevant account references, steps taken and requested assistance.

Limitations and safeguards

State estimates as estimates and separate your own observations from what another person told you.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
