Planning Follow-Up After Filing a Fraud Report

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Practical first steps

A follow-up plan keeps case numbers, deadlines and new evidence organized; response times differ by organization and jurisdiction.

Verify and preserve

Record case number, submission date, contact channel, requested information and follow-up date; log new evidence and its relevance.

Protect yourself

Do not send sensitive documents to unverified contacts; a report does not guarantee investigation or recovery.

Checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information.
  • Document reports and reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
