Connecting an Exchange Withdrawal to a Blockchain Transaction

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

An exchange withdrawal record may contain a reference that can be compared with the public-chain transaction.

Recommended lawful workflow

Record the platform case ID, withdrawal timestamp and zone, asset, network, amount, fee and transaction hash.

Limitations and safeguards

A match can confirm a transaction record but may not establish who controlled the destination wallet.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and transaction hashes.
  • Record the network, amount, timestamps and time zone.
  • Separate verified facts from assumptions.
  • Use official exchange, law-enforcement and legal channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
