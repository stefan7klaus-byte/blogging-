Red Flags in a Crypto Investigation Contract

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Take the practical first steps

A clear agreement should describe the work, deliverables, fees, timeline, privacy practices, cancellation terms and limits of the service. Read the contract calmly and request clarification before paying.

2. Preserve and verify

Be cautious if a provider promises guaranteed recovery, claims special access to private wallets, demands immediate payment, refuses to identify the contracting entity or asks for wallet secrets.

3. Protect yourself from further harm

Verify credentials and the provider's legal identity independently. Consider professional legal advice before signing a material agreement, and do not assume a contract can guarantee that third parties will freeze or return assets.

Practical checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information or engaging a provider.
  • Document reports and follow-up reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
