Wallet investigation workflows

Starting a Wallet Investigation With a Clear Scope

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A useful wallet review begins with a specific question, authorized records and a defined time range.

Recommended workflow

Write down the question, networks, known addresses, relevant dates, available records and exclusions before exploring transactions.

Limitations and safeguards

A scope limits wasted effort and helps prevent unsupported conclusions about wallet ownership.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record the network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
