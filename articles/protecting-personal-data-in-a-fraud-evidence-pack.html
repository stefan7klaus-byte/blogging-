Protecting Personal Data in a Fraud Evidence Pack

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Take the practical first steps

Before sharing an evidence pack, remove unrelated personal information from a separate copy and share only the records needed for the recipient's task. Keep unredacted originals secure and unchanged.

2. Preserve and verify

Check documents and screenshots for addresses, account numbers, identity documents, private conversations and metadata that may reveal more than intended. Use the recipient's verified secure submission method and ask how files will be stored.

3. Protect yourself from further harm

Do not upload seed phrases, private keys or passwords as 'evidence.' A legitimate review of public blockchain activity does not require those secrets. Keep a log of what you shared, with whom and when.

Practical checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information or engaging a provider.
  • Document reports and follow-up reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
