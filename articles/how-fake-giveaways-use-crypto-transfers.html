How Fake Giveaways Use Crypto Transfers

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Understand the warning signs

Fake giveaways may promise a prize or multiply a deposit but require the victim to send crypto first. They can imitate brands or influencers to create urgency.

2. Preserve relevant records

Save the original post URL, profile handle, exact claim, date, payment instructions, wallet address and transaction hash if a transfer occurred. Report the post through the platform's real tools.

3. Take safer next steps

Legitimate promotions should be independently verifiable and should not require wallet secrets. Do not send a second payment to unlock a supposed prize.

Practical checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and relevant records.
  • Separate observable facts from assumptions.
  • Protect account credentials and personal information.
  • Use official channels and independently verify providers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
