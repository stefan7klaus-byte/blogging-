Case Study: Separating Two Similar Transactions

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Two transfers with similar amounts and timestamps can look related while belonging to different events.

Recommended workflow

Compare hashes, chain IDs, token contracts, participants, fee patterns and documented context before grouping records.

Limitations and safeguards

Similarity is a lead for review, not proof that transactions share an origin.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
