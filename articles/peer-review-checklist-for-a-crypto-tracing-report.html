Preparing reports for professional review

Peer Review Checklist for a Crypto Tracing Report

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A second reviewer can identify unsupported links, missing caveats or arithmetic errors before a report is shared.

Recommended workflow

Check every material statement against its cited record, recompute totals, verify network identifiers and inspect uncertainty language.

Limitations and safeguards

Review improves quality but cannot guarantee a particular outcome or establish legal admissibility.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
