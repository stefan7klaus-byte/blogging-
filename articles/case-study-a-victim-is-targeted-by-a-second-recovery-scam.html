Case Study: A Victim Is Targeted by a Second Recovery Scam

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

After an initial loss, a victim may receive unsolicited offers promising to retrieve assets for an advance payment.

Recommended workflow

Keep the outreach, claimed credentials, fee demands and wallet destinations; avoid sending further funds or secrets.

Limitations and safeguards

No tracing service can guarantee that funds will be returned.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record the network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
