Building a Consistent Address Research Worksheet

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Understand the situation

Use a consistent worksheet with one row per address or transaction and fields for network, identifier, source URL, date checked, observed facts, assumptions and open questions.

2. Record the relevant details

Keep raw observations separate from interpretation, use consistent date and amount formats, and note whether a value came from an explorer or a third party.

3. Interpret responsibly

The worksheet is an organizational aid, not proof of attribution. Mark unknowns explicitly and do not infer that addresses belong to the same person without independent support.

Practical checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and relevant records.
  • Separate observable facts from assumptions.
  • Protect account credentials and personal information.
  • Use official channels and independently verify providers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
