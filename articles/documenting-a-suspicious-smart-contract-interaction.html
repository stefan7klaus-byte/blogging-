Ethical crypto recovery

Documenting a Suspicious Smart Contract Interaction

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Contract interactions can provide technical clues about how tokens or permissions were moved.

Recommended lawful workflow

Record the network, contract address, transaction hash, method or event details and relevant token approvals.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not interact with an unknown contract or connect a wallet merely to investigate it.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and transaction hashes.
  • Record the network, amount, timestamp and time zone.
  • Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
  • Use official platform, legal and law-enforcement channels.
  • Never share wallet seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
