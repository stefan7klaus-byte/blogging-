Impersonation Scams: Preserve the Clues

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Identify and record the warning signs

Save the profile URL, username, display name, message history, email address and any phone number used. Record copied branding, mismatched domains and payment instructions without contacting the suspected impersonator further.

2. Preserve supporting records

Keep messages in their original context and record the date, time zone and platform. Compare claimed identities only against independently sourced official contact details; lookalike names and logos are not verification.

3. Choose safer next steps

Report the profile to the relevant platform and notify the person or organization being impersonated using a verified channel. Do not share identity documents or account codes with the suspected account.

Practical checklist

  • Save original messages, URLs, receipts and transaction IDs.
  • Record dates, amounts and platform reference numbers.
  • Keep original files unchanged and store copies securely.
  • Use verified official channels to report the incident.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
