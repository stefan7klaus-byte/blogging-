Reviewing a Case File for Missing Evidence

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A completeness review identifies gaps before a report is shared with an adviser or authority.

Recommended workflow

Check for missing dates, transaction hashes, original URLs, provider communications, fee records, and a list of actions already taken.

Limitations and safeguards

Only collect records that are relevant and lawfully available; minimize unnecessary personal data.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
