Reconciling Exchange Records With On-Chain Transactions

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Exchange ledgers and public-chain transactions describe related but different views of activity.

Recommended workflow

Match timestamps, asset, amount, network, transaction hash and withdrawal reference where available; list unmatched entries separately.

Limitations and safeguards

An exchange ledger entry may be an internal movement and not a public blockchain transaction.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
