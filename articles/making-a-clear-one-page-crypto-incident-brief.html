Making a Clear One-Page Crypto Incident Brief

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Practical first steps

A one-page brief helps an exchange, adviser or reporting body understand essential facts without searching through a large folder.

Verify and preserve

Summarize the event, dates, affected services, transaction hashes and networks, loss calculation basis, actions taken and evidence index.

Protect yourself

Distinguish confirmed amounts from estimates and share sensitive records only through verified channels.

Checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information.
  • Document reports and reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
