Wallet tracing methods

Tracing Token Transfers Without Confusing Similar Assets

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Different tokens can share names, symbols or logos while using distinct contracts.

Recommended workflow

Confirm network and contract address, decimals, event log, sender, recipient and raw amount before aggregating.

Limitations and safeguards

A matching ticker is not evidence that two assets are the same.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, timestamps and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
