Recognizing Romance Scams That Introduce Cryptocurrency

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A scammer may build trust before pressuring a target to invest through a platform they control.

Practical steps

Keep dated messages, profile URLs, investment instructions, deposits and any threats or demands.

Limitations and cautions

Avoid sending money to unverified contacts, especially when asked to keep an investment secret.

Responsible checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
