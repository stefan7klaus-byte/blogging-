Recognizing Fake Crypto Support Accounts

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Understand the warning signs

Fraudsters pose as wallet or exchange support, offering help in exchange for sensitive details or fees.

Preserve relevant records

Preserve handle, profile URL, conversation, date, claimed affiliation and payment request; verify support through the official app or site.

Take safer next steps

Real support should not ask for seed phrases or private keys; avoid remote-access tools from unsolicited helpers.

Checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and original records.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Protect personal information and account credentials.
  • Use official reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
