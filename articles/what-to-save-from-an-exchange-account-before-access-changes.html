What to Save From an Exchange Account Before Access Changes

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Relevant account records can become harder to retrieve after a lock, closure or policy change.

Recommended workflow

Export available statements, deposit and withdrawal history, case messages, verification notices and fee details; retain original files.

Limitations and safeguards

Only access accounts you are authorized to use and follow the platform's terms.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record the network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
