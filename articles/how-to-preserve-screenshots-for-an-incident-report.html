How to Preserve Screenshots for an Incident Report

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Gather the relevant details

Capture enough of the screen to show the relevant content and its context, including the page or account identifier and visible date where possible. Keep the original image file and note when it was captured.

2. Preserve and organize records

Use a consistent filename and record the URL, platform, date and time zone in an evidence log. Do not crop or annotate the original; create a separate copy if highlighting is needed.

3. Review before sharing

A screenshot may omit hidden metadata or earlier messages. Preserve original exports or messages where available, and do not alter an image in a way that changes its meaning.

Practical checklist

  • Use consistent dates and time zones.
  • Preserve originals and document working copies.
  • Link key statements to specific evidence items.
  • Protect personal and account information.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
