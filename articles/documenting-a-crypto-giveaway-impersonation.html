Documenting a Crypto Giveaway Impersonation

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Giveaway impersonators may copy a public figure or brand and ask visitors to send funds first.

Recommended workflow

Capture the profile URL, exact offer, payment address, timestamps and any messages; compare the account with independently verified official channels.

Limitations and safeguards

A copied name or image does not establish that the genuine person or organization is involved.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, timestamps and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
