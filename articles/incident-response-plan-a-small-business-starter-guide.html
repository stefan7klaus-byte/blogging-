Why this topic matters

Public information still deserves privacy-aware handling. Verify who published a record, when it was captured, whether it may have changed, and what it actually proves. When applying these ideas, prioritize safety, authorization, privacy, and clear documentation.

A safe and responsible workflow

  • Define a lawful purpose
  • prefer primary sources
  • record capture times and URLs
  • preserve originals
  • minimize personal data
  • separate confirmed observations from assumptions.

Common mistakes to avoid

Do not assume a username proves identity, treat one source as conclusive, alter original evidence, or publish sensitive details unnecessarily. During an active incident, prioritize safety and response.

Evidence, documentation, and next steps

Keep a timeline, source register, chain-of-custody notes when appropriate, and concise decision summary. Transfer evidence securely to authorized recipients.
