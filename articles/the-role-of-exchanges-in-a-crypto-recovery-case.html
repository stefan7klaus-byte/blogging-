Lawful crypto recovery options

The Role of Exchanges in a Crypto Recovery Case

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A centralized exchange may be able to review deposits or accounts when it receives a credible report and lawful request.

Recommended lawful workflow

Identify the network and transaction hash, collect the platform's official reporting instructions and retain the case reference.

Limitations and safeguards

An exchange cannot necessarily freeze funds or disclose customer information on request.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and transaction hashes.
  • Record the network, amount, timestamps and time zone.
  • Separate verified facts from assumptions.
  • Use official exchange, law-enforcement and legal channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
