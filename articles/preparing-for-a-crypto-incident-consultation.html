Preparing for a Crypto Incident Consultation

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

An organized case summary helps a qualified adviser understand the event and identify missing information.

Practical steps

Prepare a timeline, transaction hashes, networks, payment records, provider communications, prior actions and questions about costs and scope.

Limitations and cautions

Share sensitive evidence only through verified secure channels.

Responsible checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
