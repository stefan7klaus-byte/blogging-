Understanding Exchange Withdrawal Status Labels

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Statuses such as pending, processing, completed or failed are platform-defined and may not mean the same thing across providers.

Recommended workflow

Save the exact status text, timestamp, network and transaction hash, and compare it with public chain data when a hash exists.

Limitations and safeguards

A completed platform status may require separate confirmation that the on-chain transaction occurred.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record the network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
