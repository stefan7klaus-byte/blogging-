Reconstructing a Timeline Across Time Zones

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Exchange logs, chats and blockchain explorers may display timestamps in different time zones or formats.

Recommended workflow

Keep original timestamps, note each source's time zone, and add a normalized UTC column only with a clear conversion method.

Limitations and safeguards

Daylight-saving transitions and missing time-zone metadata can cause errors.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
