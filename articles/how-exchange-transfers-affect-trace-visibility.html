Blockchain networks and tracing limitations

How Exchange Transfers Affect Trace Visibility

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Identify what the source displays

When funds move through an exchange or other custodial service, the public ledger may show a deposit address or service-controlled wallet rather than the service's internal customer records. Internal allocation records are not generally visible on-chain.

2. Capture the details reproducibly

Record the visible deposit or withdrawal transaction, relevant timestamps, amounts, network and any exchange case number. Contact the service through its official channel to ask what information can be reviewed or disclosed through its process.

3. Avoid unsupported conclusions

Do not assume an address label identifies a specific customer, and do not attempt to access another person's account. The service may require consent or formal legal process to disclose account-level information.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm the network and exact transaction identifier.
  • Save the source URL and date checked.
  • Separate direct observations from analysis.
  • Record uncertainty and alternative explanations.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
