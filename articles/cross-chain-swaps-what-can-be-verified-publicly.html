Cross-Chain Swaps: What Can Be Verified Publicly

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Cross-chain swaps may involve multiple contracts, services or separate transaction records.

Recommended workflow

Record each network, transaction hash, contract, amount, timestamps and any public swap identifier; map only supported links.

Limitations and safeguards

Some swaps do not expose a simple public one-to-one transaction match.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, timestamps and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
