Blockchain networks and tracing limitations

How Network Congestion Can Delay Transaction Confirmation

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Understand the network

Network congestion, fee settings and protocol rules can affect how quickly a transaction is included or finalized.

Record relevant details

Record the hash, broadcast or request time if known, fee details, network status and repeated checks with timestamps.

Recognize the limits

Delay alone does not prove theft or failure; do not resend funds without understanding the wallet's current transaction state.

Checklist

  • Confirm the network and exact identifier.
  • Keep source URLs and dates checked.
  • Separate direct observations from analysis.
  • Document uncertainty and alternative explanations.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
