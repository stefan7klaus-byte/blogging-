Preparing a Follow-Up After an Initial Fraud Report

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A focused follow-up gives the receiving organization a clear reference to the original report and any new evidence.

Recommended workflow

Quote the case reference, summarize new verified facts, attach only relevant records and ask one or two specific questions.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not send repeated bulk messages or assume a lack of response means a case has been accepted.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
