Distinguishing Pending and Confirmed Transactions

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Understand the displayed data

A pending transaction may not yet be included in a block; a confirmed transaction has been included according to the explorer's displayed status. Network conditions and service policies affect how statuses are presented.

2. Record the source

Record the status and time checked, then recheck through a reputable explorer if the status matters to your report. If an exchange shows a separate deposit status, note it independently from the blockchain status.

3. Interpret with care

Do not interpret a pending transaction as completed payment, and do not assume a confirmation means a recipient's service account has been credited. Preserve the exact status message and its source.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm the network and asset.
  • Record the transaction ID and source URL.
  • Keep original values alongside any conversions.
  • Separate observable data from assumptions.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
