Writing a Neutral Crypto Incident Report

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A factual report helps recipients assess events without confusing observations with conclusions about identity or intent.

Practical steps

Use dates, source references, transaction IDs and explicit uncertainty labels; quote exact wording when relevant.

Limitations and cautions

Avoid public accusations based only on wallet addresses, usernames or unexplained transaction patterns.

Responsible checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
