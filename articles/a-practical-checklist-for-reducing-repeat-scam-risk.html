A Practical Checklist for Reducing Repeat Scam Risk

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Take practical, proportionate steps

Use unique passwords and strong multi-factor authentication, keep devices updated, verify URLs before signing in, and confirm investment claims independently. Never send assets because a stranger promises guaranteed returns.

2. Prepare and verify

Keep a simple incident-response folder with transaction IDs, receipts, messages, account case numbers and a dated timeline. Store backups securely and review account access after any suspected compromise.

3. Set realistic expectations

Before paying for professional help, verify the provider, compare written scopes and fees, and take time to review the terms. Report incidents through official channels and remember that tracing does not guarantee recovery.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve relevant records and source links.
  • Use official channels and verify identities independently.
  • Keep original evidence secure and unchanged.
  • Review written scopes and costs before engaging a provider.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
