Blockchain networks and tracing limitations

Understanding Bridge Transactions and Cross-Chain Handoffs

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Understand the network

A cross-chain transfer may involve a source-chain lock or burn and a destination-chain mint or release, with different transaction identifiers.

Record relevant details

Preserve source and destination hashes, chain names, bridge service, timestamps, token contract and status from each side.

Recognize the limits

A related-looking destination transfer needs verification; bridges can fail, delay or use complex intermediary steps.

Checklist

  • Confirm the network and exact identifier.
  • Keep source URLs and dates checked.
  • Separate direct observations from analysis.
  • Document uncertainty and alternative explanations.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
