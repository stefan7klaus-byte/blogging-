Creating a Source Register for Digital Evidence

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A source register gives each record a stable reference so a reader can trace a report statement back to evidence.

Recommended workflow

Assign evidence IDs and record origin, collector, acquisition date, file name, hash where appropriate and access restrictions.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not expose sensitive personal information in public copies of the register.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
