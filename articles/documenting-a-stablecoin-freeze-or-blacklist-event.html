Exchange records and stablecoin transfers

Documenting a Stablecoin Freeze or Blacklist Event

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Some token issuers or contracts may have administrative controls that affect particular addresses.

Recommended workflow

Record the token contract, chain, relevant transaction or public notice, date and official issuer documentation.

Limitations and safeguards

Controls vary by token and jurisdiction; do not promise an issuer will freeze or return funds.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
