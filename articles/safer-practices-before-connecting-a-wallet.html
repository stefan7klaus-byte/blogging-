Safer Practices Before Connecting a Wallet

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Take practical first steps

Only connect a wallet to websites you independently trust and understand. Review requested permissions and transaction details before approving; reject unexpected signature or token-approval prompts.

2. Verify and preserve

Use a separate, limited-purpose wallet for experimentation where appropriate, keep software updated, and review token approvals through a trusted workflow. Never paste a seed phrase into a website or support chat.

3. Protect yourself from further harm

A connection or signature can have consequences beyond viewing a page. If a request is unclear, stop and verify it through official documentation or qualified support.

Practical checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information or engaging a provider.
  • Document reports and follow-up reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
