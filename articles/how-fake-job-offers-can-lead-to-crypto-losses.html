How Fake Job Offers Can Lead to Crypto Losses

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Recognize the warning signs

Fraudulent job offers may ask applicants to deposit funds, buy crypto, or use personal accounts to move money.

Preserve relevant records

Preserve the job listing, recruiter details, contract, messages and payment requests; independently verify the employer.

Take safer next steps

Do not forward funds for strangers or pay to access a job; these requests can expose you to fraud or legal risk.

Checklist

  • Keep original records, URLs and dates.
  • Verify claims through independent official channels.
  • Separate facts from assumptions.
  • Protect personal data and account credentials.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
