Checklist for Securing an Exchange Account

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Take the practical first steps

Use a unique password stored in a reputable password manager and enable strong multi-factor authentication. Review recent logins, active sessions, withdrawal settings and approved API keys using the exchange's official app or website.

2. Preserve and verify

Secure the email account associated with the exchange too, check recovery details, and revoke sessions or keys you do not recognize. Save relevant alerts and account activity records before they disappear, if safe to do so.

3. Protect yourself from further harm

Never share a password, one-time code, private key or seed phrase with someone claiming to be support. If you suspect account compromise, use the provider's official account-security process promptly.

Practical checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information or engaging a provider.
  • Document reports and follow-up reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
