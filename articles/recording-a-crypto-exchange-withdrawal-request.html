Recording a Crypto Exchange Withdrawal Request

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Why this matters

An exchange withdrawal request and an on-chain transaction are separate events and may have different timestamps or statuses.

Practical steps

Save the activity entry, asset, amount, destination, request ID, status and any resulting transaction hash.

Limitations and care

Describe the exact interface status rather than assuming it means on-chain finality.

Checklist

  • Keep originals unchanged and store them securely.
  • Record source, date and time zone.
  • Separate confirmed facts from estimates.
  • Share only relevant information through verified channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
