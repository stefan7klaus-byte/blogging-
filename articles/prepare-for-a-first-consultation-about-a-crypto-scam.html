Prepare for a First Consultation About a Crypto Scam

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Take practical, proportionate steps

Prepare a concise timeline, transaction hashes, wallet addresses, receipts, relevant messages and records of reports already submitted. List what is confirmed, what remains uncertain and the questions you need answered.

2. Prepare and verify

Ask about the provider's process, scope, qualifications, fees, privacy safeguards, reporting options and realistic limitations. Share only the minimum relevant information through a verified channel and avoid sending credentials.

3. Set realistic expectations

A first consultation should help you understand options, not pressure you into an immediate payment. Verify the provider independently and consider qualified legal or official reporting guidance when appropriate.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve relevant records and source links.
  • Use official channels and verify identities independently.
  • Keep original evidence secure and unchanged.
  • Review written scopes and costs before engaging a provider.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
