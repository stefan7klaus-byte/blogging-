Writing a Clear Evidence Request to a Crypto Exchange

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A precise request helps support staff identify the relevant account or transaction without unnecessary back-and-forth.

Recommended workflow

Include the date range, network, transaction hash, amount, withdrawal reference and a concise question; use official support channels.

Limitations and safeguards

An exchange may be unable to disclose customer information without valid legal process.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record the network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
