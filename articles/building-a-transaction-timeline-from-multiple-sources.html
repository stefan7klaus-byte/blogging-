Building a Transaction Timeline From Multiple Sources

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A useful timeline combines on-chain events with communications and platform records without blending their different evidentiary weight.

Recommended workflow

Create a row per event with timestamp, time zone, source, hash or reference, observed fact and confidence note.

Limitations and safeguards

Keep original timestamps and identify any estimated or converted times.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, timestamps and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
