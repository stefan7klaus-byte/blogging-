How to Evaluate a Crypto Forensics Report

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A useful report makes its methods, sources and uncertainties easy to audit.

Recommended lawful workflow

Check transaction hashes, network identification, timestamps, address labels, assumptions, limitations and reproducibility.

Limitations and safeguards

Treat unsupported identity claims and guaranteed recovery promises as red flags.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and transaction hashes.
  • Record network, amounts, timestamps and time zones.
  • Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
  • Use official platform, legal and law-enforcement channels.
  • Never share wallet seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
