Documenting a Suspicious Trading Website

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Verify the claim

Record the full domain, page URLs, date visited, contact details, claimed company name and any suspicious requests. Save screenshots that show context and preserve downloaded documents without editing the originals.

2. Preserve useful records

Create a short note describing how you found the site and what made it suspicious. Keep transaction references, support tickets and any emails in a secure evidence folder, with original files separated from annotated copies.

3. Take safer next steps

A suspicious appearance is a lead, not a final determination. Avoid logging in again or downloading unknown files; report the site through your browser, hosting provider, relevant platform or appropriate authority.

Practical checklist

  • Keep messages, URLs, receipts and transaction IDs.
  • Record dates, amounts and report reference numbers.
  • Preserve original files securely.
  • Use independently verified official channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
