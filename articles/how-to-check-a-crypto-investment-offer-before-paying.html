How to Check a Crypto Investment Offer Before Paying

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Verify the claim

Pause before transferring funds. Verify the company's legal name, domain, contact details, registration claims and withdrawal terms using independent sources. Do not rely on a promoter's screenshots or testimonials alone.

2. Preserve useful records

Save the advertisement, website address, terms, chat messages, wallet instructions, receipts and transaction IDs. Note what was promised, what you checked and any details that could not be verified.

3. Take safer next steps

A polished site and visible account balance do not establish legitimacy. Avoid borrowing or sending more money to meet a deadline, and contact your bank, exchange or payment service through verified channels if you suspect fraud.

Practical checklist

  • Keep messages, URLs, receipts and transaction IDs.
  • Record dates, amounts and report reference numbers.
  • Preserve original files securely.
  • Use independently verified official channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
