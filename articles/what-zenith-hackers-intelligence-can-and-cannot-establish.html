Networks, limitations and safety

What Zenith Hackers Intelligence Can and Cannot Establish

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A responsible enquiry should set expectations around public-chain analysis, private platform records and real-world attribution.

Recommended workflow

Prepare relevant transaction hashes, network details, timeline and specific questions; ask for scope, methods, deliverables and limitations in writing.

Limitations and safeguards

Public blockchain data alone may not identify a person or guarantee recovery.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, timestamps and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
