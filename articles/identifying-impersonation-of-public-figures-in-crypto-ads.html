Identifying Impersonation of Public Figures in Crypto Ads

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Recognize the warning signs

Scammers can misuse public figures' names, images or interviews to promote fake tokens and investment schemes.

Preserve relevant records

Save the ad, account name, URL, date, claimed endorsement and payment instructions; verify announcements on official channels.

Take safer next steps

A copied photo or video does not prove endorsement; avoid decisions based on sponsored posts alone.

Checklist

  • Keep original records, URLs and dates.
  • Verify claims through independent official channels.
  • Separate facts from assumptions.
  • Protect personal data and account credentials.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
