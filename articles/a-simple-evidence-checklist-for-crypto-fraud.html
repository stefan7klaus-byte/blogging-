A Simple Evidence Checklist for Crypto Fraud

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Gather the relevant details

Begin with a concise inventory: transaction IDs, wallet addresses, network, dates, amounts, receipts, messages, platform URLs and any report reference numbers. Include only records relevant to the incident.

2. Preserve and organize records

Keep the original items unchanged, make a working copy for notes, and record each item's source and collection date. Store sensitive records in a secure location and maintain a backup with limited access.

3. Review before sharing

A checklist organizes evidence but does not prove the claim by itself. Identify missing records honestly and separate your own recollection from facts supported by a third-party record.

Practical checklist

  • Use consistent dates and time zones.
  • Preserve originals and document working copies.
  • Link key statements to specific evidence items.
  • Protect personal and account information.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
