How to Set Measurable Goals for a Crypto Tracing Engagement

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A well-scoped engagement focuses on verifiable deliverables rather than promises of returned funds.

Recommended lawful workflow

Agree on the networks and transactions to review, reporting format, source citations, timeline, budget and limitations in writing.

Limitations and safeguards

A successful analysis does not necessarily mean assets will be recovered.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and transaction hashes.
  • Record the network, amount, timestamps and time zone.
  • Separate verified facts from assumptions.
  • Use official exchange, law-enforcement and legal channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
