Documenting a Crypto Impersonation Scam

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Impersonators may copy a company's name, profile picture or branding to create false trust.

Recommended workflow

Preserve profile URL, account handle, message export, timestamps, payment request and any claimed affiliation.

Limitations and safeguards

Copied branding does not establish that the genuine organization was involved.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, timestamps and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
