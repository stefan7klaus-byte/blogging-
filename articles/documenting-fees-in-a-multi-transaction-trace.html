Documenting Fees in a Multi-Transaction Trace

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Network fees, service fees and swap differences can explain why amounts change along a path.

Recommended workflow

Record each fee separately with its asset, amount, transaction and source; show calculations and rounding assumptions.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not assume every value difference is a fee.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, timestamps and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
