Build a Clear Timeline After a Cryptocurrency Scam

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Build a clear record

Build a timeline with the date, time zone, event, account or service involved, amount or asset, supporting record and action taken. Keep the entries chronological and use exact dates where available.

2. Preserve the source material

Link each entry to a message, receipt, transaction ID, email or case number. Note whether a time came from a platform display, your own recollection or another source, and avoid changing the original records.

3. Review for accuracy and limits

A timeline helps a reviewer follow the sequence but does not prove every claim by itself. Mark gaps clearly, distinguish direct observations from interpretations, and update the timeline with dated corrections rather than silently overwriting earlier entries.

Practical checklist

  • Use consistent dates and time zones.
  • Keep original records unchanged and secure.
  • Link factual statements to supporting records.
  • Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
