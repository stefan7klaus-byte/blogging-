How to Protect Personal Data During a Recovery Enquiry

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A case file can contain identity documents, account details, financial records and private communications.

Recommended lawful workflow

Share only relevant records, redact unnecessary identifiers, use a verified secure channel and ask how files are stored and deleted.

Limitations and safeguards

Never include passwords, authentication codes, private keys or wallet recovery phrases.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and transaction hashes.
  • Record the network, amount, timestamps and time zone.
  • Separate verified facts from assumptions.
  • Use official exchange, law-enforcement and legal channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
