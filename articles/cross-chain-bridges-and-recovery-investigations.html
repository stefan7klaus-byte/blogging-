Cross-Chain Bridges and Recovery Investigations

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Bridges can move assets between networks, complicating a trace across transaction systems.

Recommended lawful workflow

Record the source-chain transaction, bridge contract or service, destination network and any linked destination transaction.

Limitations and safeguards

Bridging can make tracking harder; matching amounts or timing alone is not conclusive attribution.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and transaction hashes.
  • Record network, amounts, timestamps and time zones.
  • Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
  • Use official platform, legal and law-enforcement channels.
  • Never share wallet seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
