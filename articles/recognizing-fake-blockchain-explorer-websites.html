Recognizing Fake Blockchain Explorer Websites

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Fraudulent sites may imitate legitimate explorers to collect credentials or display fabricated transaction status.

Recommended workflow

Navigate through a trusted bookmark or independently verified domain; compare the transaction hash on the official network explorer and avoid entering wallet secrets.

Limitations and safeguards

A site showing a transaction or balance is not proof that it is genuine.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
