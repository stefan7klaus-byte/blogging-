What a Block Height Tells You

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Understand the situation

Block height is a block's position in a chain. It can help locate a transaction and compare records, but it is not the same as a calendar timestamp.

2. Record the relevant details

Record block height with transaction hash, network, timestamp as displayed and date checked. Confirm the block belongs to the intended network.

3. Interpret responsibly

Block height cannot establish a user's identity, location or intent. Avoid treating an explorer's display convention as universal across all chains.

Practical checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and relevant records.
  • Separate observable facts from assumptions.
  • Protect account credentials and personal information.
  • Use official channels and independently verify providers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
