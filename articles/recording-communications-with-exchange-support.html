Evidence collection and reporting

Recording Communications With Exchange Support

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Why this matters

Support conversations may document a service's stated status, troubleshooting and case references.

Practical steps

Save the relevant thread, ticket ID, dates, channel and promised follow-up; verify the support channel is official.

Limitations and care

Informal support statements are not guarantees; never share passwords, codes, private keys or seed phrases.

Checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and original records.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Protect personal information and account credentials.
  • Use official reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
