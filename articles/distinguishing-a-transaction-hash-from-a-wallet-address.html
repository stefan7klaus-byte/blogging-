Distinguishing a Transaction Hash From a Wallet Address

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

What to understand

A transaction hash identifies a particular transaction, while a wallet address is used to receive or control assets on a network.

Practical steps

Label each identifier by type and network, preserve its original text and check it in the appropriate explorer.

Important limitations

A valid-looking string may belong to another network or identifier type; do not assume formats are universally interchangeable.

Checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record the network and date/time checked.
  • Separate observed facts from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
