Interpreting Exchange Labels in Blockchain Explorers

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Explorer labels can be useful leads but may be incomplete, outdated or based on third-party reports.

Recommended workflow

Save the label, date checked, source and any corroborating official documentation; phrase the result as a sourced label rather than certainty.

Limitations and safeguards

A label does not establish who initiated a specific transaction or why.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
