Understanding Ethereum Transaction Receipts

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Identify what the source displays

An Ethereum transaction receipt can show whether a transaction succeeded or failed, along with block information and execution-related fields. Token transfers and contract events may require reviewing logs or the explorer's separate events section.

2. Capture the details reproducibly

Record the transaction hash, status, block, sender and recipient fields, and relevant token events or contract addresses. Save the explorer URL and distinguish a top-level transaction value from any token transfer amounts.

3. Avoid unsupported conclusions

A successful contract call does not guarantee that the user's intended business outcome occurred. Interpret the event in context and do not infer personal identity or intent from the receipt alone.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm the network and exact transaction identifier.
  • Save the source URL and date checked.
  • Separate direct observations from analysis.
  • Record uncertainty and alternative explanations.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
