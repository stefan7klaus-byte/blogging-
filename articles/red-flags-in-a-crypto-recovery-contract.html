Red Flags in a Crypto Recovery Contract

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Vague deliverables, hidden fees and guarantees can leave victims exposed to further losses.

Recommended lawful workflow

Request a written scope, fee schedule, privacy terms, cancellation policy, realistic limitations and a description of the evidence you'll receive.

Limitations and safeguards

Consider independent legal advice before signing or paying substantial fees.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and transaction hashes.
  • Record the network, amount, timestamps and time zone.
  • Separate verified facts from assumptions.
  • Use official exchange, law-enforcement and legal channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
