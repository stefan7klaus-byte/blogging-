How to Verify Official Crypto Support Channels

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Take the practical first steps

Open the platform's official website by typing a known address or using a trusted bookmark, then navigate to its support section from there. Treat unsolicited direct messages, search ads and lookalike domains with caution.

2. Preserve and verify

Record the exact account, URL and message that contacted you. Compare the contact method with the platform's published official help information, and report suspected impersonation through the real service.

3. Protect yourself from further harm

A logo or verified-looking profile image is not sufficient proof. Never click a login link from an unsolicited message or provide credentials, recovery codes or wallet secrets to a stranger.

Practical checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information or engaging a provider.
  • Document reports and follow-up reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
