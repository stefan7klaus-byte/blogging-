Validating a Suspected Bridge Transaction Pair

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A candidate bridge pair should be supported by matching records rather than similar amounts alone.

Recommended workflow

Compare networks, hashes, bridge identifiers, token contracts, amounts and timestamps; document each matching field and mismatch.

Limitations and safeguards

If the bridge does not expose a reliable correlation identifier, mark the relationship as tentative.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
