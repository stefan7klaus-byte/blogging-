What to Do After Sending Crypto to a Scammer

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Verify the claim

Stop contact with the suspected scammer and do not send more funds for supposed taxes, unlocking fees or recovery. Contact the exchange, wallet provider, bank or payment service involved as soon as possible using official channels.

2. Preserve useful records

Collect the transaction hash, network, amount, destination address, date and time, receipts, messages and any account or case reference. Ask the provider what information it needs and retain the response and report number.

3. Take safer next steps

Blockchain transfers are generally not reversible through a simple chargeback, but prompt reporting may help providers assess the incident. Be wary of anyone guaranteeing recovery or asking for your seed phrase or private key.

Practical checklist

  • Keep messages, URLs, receipts and transaction IDs.
  • Record dates, amounts and report reference numbers.
  • Preserve original files securely.
  • Use independently verified official channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
