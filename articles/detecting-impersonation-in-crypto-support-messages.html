Detecting Impersonation in Crypto Support Messages

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Scammers may copy company branding, staff names and support language to appear legitimate.

Recommended workflow

Record sender identifiers, message headers where available, URLs and payment instructions; verify contact details independently.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not reply through the suspicious link or share credentials to prove account ownership.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
