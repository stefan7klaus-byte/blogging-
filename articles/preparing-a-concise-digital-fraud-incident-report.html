Preparing a Concise Digital-Fraud Incident Report

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Build a clear record

A concise report should state what happened, when it happened, what assets or payment channels were involved, the transaction identifiers, the records preserved and the actions already taken.

2. Preserve the source material

Organize the report with a one-page summary, a dated timeline, an evidence index and copies of key records. Separate confirmed facts from estimates and open questions; use clear filenames and keep sensitive originals secure.

3. Review for accuracy and limits

Avoid unsupported conclusions, inflated loss estimates or promises of recovery. Keep the report factual and ask the recipient what additional evidence or reporting format is needed.

Practical checklist

  • Use consistent dates and time zones.
  • Keep original records unchanged and secure.
  • Link factual statements to supporting records.
  • Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
