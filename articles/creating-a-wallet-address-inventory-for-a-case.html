Creating a Wallet Address Inventory for a Case

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

An address inventory prevents similarly formatted identifiers from being confused across networks or records.

Recommended workflow

Assign each address a neutral ID and record its network, source, first-seen date, relevant hashes and confidence in any association.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not label an address as belonging to a named person without reliable supporting evidence.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record the network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
