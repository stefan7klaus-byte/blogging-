What to Include in a Crypto Scam Evidence Index

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

An index helps a reviewer find each supporting record without reading every file first.

Recommended workflow

Assign an evidence ID, filename, description, source, date collected, relevant transaction or event and any redaction status.

Limitations and safeguards

Avoid including secrets or unrelated personal information in the index.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
