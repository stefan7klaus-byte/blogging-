Creating a Timeline From Messages and Transactions

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Why this matters

A timeline connects communications with reported payments while keeping the source of each event visible.

Practical steps

Use columns for date/time and time zone, event, source record, transaction reference and confidence; mark estimates explicitly.

Limitations and care

A timeline organizes evidence but does not independently prove causation or identity.

Checklist

  • Keep originals unchanged and store them securely.
  • Record source, date and time zone.
  • Separate confirmed facts from estimates.
  • Share only relevant information through verified channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
