Preparing a Timeline That Separates Reports From Chain Events

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A case timeline may combine a person's account of events, messages, platform actions and public blockchain records.

Recommended workflow

Label the source type for each event and preserve the original timestamp alongside any normalized UTC value.

Limitations and safeguards

Temporal proximity alone does not establish causation.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
