Preserving Messages and Emails as Evidence

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Build a clear record

Preserve messages in their original context, including sender identifiers, timestamps, relevant attachments and surrounding conversation. For email, retain the original message or export with headers where possible rather than only a cropped screenshot.

2. Preserve the source material

Create working copies for highlights or annotations, keep originals unchanged, and record when and how each item was collected. Store sensitive records securely and limit access to people who need them.

3. Review for accuracy and limits

Do not edit originals to make a story look clearer. If you must redact a copy for sharing, keep the unredacted original safely and label the redacted version. Platform exports and screenshots may omit metadata, so preserve both when appropriate.

Practical checklist

  • Use consistent dates and time zones.
  • Keep original records unchanged and secure.
  • Link factual statements to supporting records.
  • Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
