Reading Block Timestamps and Time Zones

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Understand the displayed data

Block timestamps are commonly represented in UTC or displayed using the browser's local time zone. Different interfaces can format the same moment differently.

2. Record the source

Save the timestamp as displayed, identify the time zone if known, and convert only when necessary while retaining the original value. Include the collection time and source URL in your notes.

3. Interpret with care

A timestamp may represent block time rather than the exact moment a person clicked a button. Avoid false precision and use related records, such as exchange receipts or messages, to build a fuller timeline.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm the network and asset.
  • Record the transaction ID and source URL.
  • Keep original values alongside any conversions.
  • Separate observable data from assumptions.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
