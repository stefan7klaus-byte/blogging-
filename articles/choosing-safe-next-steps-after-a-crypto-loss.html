Choosing Safe Next Steps After a Crypto Loss

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

After a loss, prioritize account security and evidence preservation before engaging with unsolicited helpers.

Recommended workflow

Secure email and exchange accounts, preserve transaction and message records, contact platforms through official channels and report where appropriate.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not retaliate, attempt unauthorized access or trust promises of guaranteed recovery.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
