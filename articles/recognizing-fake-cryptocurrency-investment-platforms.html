Recognizing Fake Cryptocurrency Investment Platforms

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Identify and record the warning signs

Check a platform's legal name, domain spelling, ownership disclosures, fee schedule and withdrawal terms independently. Search for regulatory warnings using official regulator sites, not links supplied by the promoter.

2. Preserve supporting records

Preserve the original advertisement, URL, chat history, account identifiers, payment instructions and any receipts. Record what returns were promised, whether withdrawals were actually permitted, and when new fees appeared.

3. Choose safer next steps

Screenshots and public reviews alone do not prove a platform is legitimate or fraudulent. Do not deposit more money to unlock a balance; contact your exchange, bank or payment provider through official channels.

Practical checklist

  • Save original messages, URLs, receipts and transaction IDs.
  • Record dates, amounts and platform reference numbers.
  • Keep original files unchanged and store copies securely.
  • Use verified official channels to report the incident.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
