Writing a Careful Blockchain Attribution Note

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Identify what the source displays

A careful attribution note separates direct observations, analytical inferences, alternative explanations and unresolved questions. For each claim, cite the source and state why it supports the claim.

2. Capture the details reproducibly

Use cautious wording such as 'the explorer displays' for direct observations and 'may be consistent with' for an unconfirmed interpretation. Record the date, network, transaction hash and any assumptions behind the analysis.

3. Avoid unsupported conclusions

Avoid naming a person or alleging control based only on an address label, timing or similar amounts. Attribution should be proportionate to the evidence and independently corroborated where possible.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm the network and exact transaction identifier.
  • Save the source URL and date checked.
  • Separate direct observations from analysis.
  • Record uncertainty and alternative explanations.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
