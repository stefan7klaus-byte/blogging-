Fraud evidence and verification

Verifying a Claimed Crypto Payment With Public Records

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A screenshot or payment receipt should be checked against the relevant blockchain or platform records.

Recommended workflow

Confirm network, transaction hash, sender and recipient, asset contract, amount, status and timestamp using a trusted source.

Limitations and safeguards

A transaction hash proves a recorded transaction, not every claim made about the payment.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, timestamps and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
