Questions to Ask About Data Security and Confidentiality

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Take practical first steps

Ask who can access your records, where they are stored, whether subcontractors are used, how long data is retained and how you can request deletion or receive a copy of your case file.

2. Verify and preserve

Request answers in writing and check whether the contract's confidentiality terms match the provider's privacy notice. Share only necessary information and use verified secure transfer methods.

3. Protect yourself from further harm

Confidentiality language does not guarantee absolute secrecy, and legal obligations may apply. Avoid sharing credentials or wallet secrets under any circumstances.

Practical checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information or engaging a provider.
  • Document reports and follow-up reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
