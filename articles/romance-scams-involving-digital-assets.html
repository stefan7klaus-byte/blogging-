Romance Scams Involving Digital Assets

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Identify and record the warning signs

Romance-investment scams can build trust over time before introducing a trading platform, wallet or supposed investment opportunity. Treat requests to transfer funds to a contact's wallet or a platform they control as a serious warning sign.

2. Preserve supporting records

Preserve the conversation history, profile links, images as received, platform URLs, payment receipts and blockchain transaction IDs. Document the sequence of events without deleting messages or editing original files.

3. Choose safer next steps

Do not send more funds to pay alleged taxes, unlock withdrawals or prove trust. Talk to a trusted person, contact financial providers promptly and report the incident through official channels. Recovery guarantees are another common warning sign.

Practical checklist

  • Save original messages, URLs, receipts and transaction IDs.
  • Record dates, amounts and platform reference numbers.
  • Keep original files unchanged and store copies securely.
  • Use verified official channels to report the incident.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
