Crypto Recovery Fraud: Warning Signs and Safe Responses

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Identify and record the warning signs

Warning signs include unsolicited contact, guaranteed recovery claims, fabricated affiliations, pressure to act immediately, advance taxes or fees, and requests for seed phrases or remote access.

2. Preserve supporting records

Preserve the pitch, website, payment details, usernames, email addresses and any contract or invoice. Verify a provider's legal identity, credentials, scope and fees through independent sources before sharing case information.

3. Choose safer next steps

Do not pay a second party promising guaranteed recovery of the first loss. Secure your accounts and report both incidents to relevant providers and authorities. Blockchain tracing may produce leads, but it cannot promise funds will be returned.

Practical checklist

  • Save original messages, URLs, receipts and transaction IDs.
  • Record dates, amounts and platform reference numbers.
  • Keep original files unchanged and store copies securely.
  • Use verified official channels to report the incident.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
