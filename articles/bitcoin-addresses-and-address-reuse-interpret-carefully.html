Bitcoin Addresses and Address Reuse: Interpret Carefully

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Understand the data

Bitcoin address reuse can make activity easier to associate on the public ledger, but one address does not automatically equal one person. Wallet software and service practices vary.

2. Record the relevant details

Record the address exactly, network, source URL and relevant transaction hashes. Note whether the address was supplied by a person, copied from a service or observed in a public transaction.

3. Avoid unsupported conclusions

Address reuse is only one observation and cannot establish legal identity or intent. Avoid conclusions about ownership without corroborating records.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm the network and exact transaction identifier.
  • Save source URLs and dates checked.
  • Separate direct observations from analysis.
  • Record uncertainty and alternative explanations.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
