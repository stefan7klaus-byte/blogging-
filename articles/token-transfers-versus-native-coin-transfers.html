Token Transfers Versus Native-Coin Transfers

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Understand the issue

Smart-contract activity can create token-transfer events even when the top-level native-coin amount is small or zero.

Preserve useful records

Record network, hash, native-coin value, token contract and token amount separately; verify contract details with reliable sources.

Interpret responsibly

A token name or symbol alone does not prove authenticity; do not treat distinct assets as interchangeable.

Practical checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and original records.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Protect account credentials and personal information.
  • Use official reporting and support channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
