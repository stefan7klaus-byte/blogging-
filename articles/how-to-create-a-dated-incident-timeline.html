How to Create a Dated Incident Timeline

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Gather the key details

Start with the earliest known event and add dated entries for messages, transfers, account changes, reports and follow-ups. Identify the time zone when known and distinguish exact timestamps from recollections.

2. Preserve and organize records

Assign each event a short label and link it to evidence filenames or transaction hashes. Keep the original record alongside any normalized time or currency conversion.

3. Review before sharing

A timeline organizes information but does not prove causation. Mark gaps and conflicting dates instead of silently choosing one version.

Practical checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and relevant records.
  • Separate observable facts from assumptions.
  • Protect account credentials and personal information.
  • Use official channels and independently verify providers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
