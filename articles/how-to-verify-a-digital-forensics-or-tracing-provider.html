How to Verify a Digital Forensics or Tracing Provider

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Practical first steps

Before hiring a specialist, establish legal identity, experience, scope, fees, privacy practices and suitability for the case.

Verify and preserve

Ask for written deliverables, limitations, data-handling terms and complaints process; verify claims independently.

Protect yourself

Beware guaranteed recovery, hidden fees, fabricated affiliations and pressure to act immediately.

Checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information.
  • Document reports and reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
