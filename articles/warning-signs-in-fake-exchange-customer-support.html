Scam investigation and fraud evidence

Warning Signs in Fake Exchange Customer Support

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Impersonators use unofficial accounts and lookalike domains to request payments or sensitive information.

Practical steps

Record account name and URL, preserve the conversation and contact the exchange through its verified website.

Limitations and cautions

Logos and caller ID can be copied; do not rely on links supplied only by a suspicious message.

Responsible checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
