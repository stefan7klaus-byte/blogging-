Keeping an Evidence Index for a Digital-Fraud Case

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Gather the relevant details

Assign each item a unique evidence number and create a table with its filename, description, source, date collected, relevance and any handling notes. Link timeline entries to the corresponding evidence numbers.

2. Preserve and organize records

Use descriptive filenames and keep a read-only copy of original material. If an item is replaced or supplemented, retain the earlier version and document the reason for the update.

3. Review before sharing

An index makes records easier to review, but it should not expose unrelated personal information. Share the minimum necessary records through a verified secure channel and follow the recipient's requirements.

Practical checklist

  • Use consistent dates and time zones.
  • Preserve originals and document working copies.
  • Link key statements to specific evidence items.
  • Protect personal and account information.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
