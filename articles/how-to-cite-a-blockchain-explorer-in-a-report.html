How to Cite a Blockchain Explorer in a Report

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Citations let another reviewer open the same transaction record and verify what was visible.

Recommended workflow

Record the explorer name, full URL, transaction hash, network and date accessed; preserve a screenshot only as a supplement.

Limitations and safeguards

Explorer pages can change, and screenshots alone do not replace the underlying record.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record the network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
