Preserving Evidence From a Suspicious Messaging Group

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Understand the warning signs

Scam groups may contain copied testimonials, fake profit screenshots and changing administrator accounts; safety comes first.

Preserve relevant records

Where safe and lawful, record group name, public link, administrator handles, message timestamps and payment instructions.

Take safer next steps

Do not expose other members' private information publicly; report suspicious groups through platform tools.

Checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and original records.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Protect personal information and account credentials.
  • Use official reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
