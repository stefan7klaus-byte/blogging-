A Safe Checklist Before Sharing Case Documents

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Take practical first steps

Before sharing documents, confirm who is receiving them, why each item is needed, how it will be transmitted and how the recipient will protect it. Use a verified secure channel where available.

2. Verify and preserve

Keep originals private, create redacted copies when appropriate, remove unrelated information, and log which files were shared and when. Never include passwords, private keys or seed phrases.

3. Protect yourself from further harm

Share the minimum necessary information. If the request comes from an unsolicited contact, independently verify the recipient before opening attachments or sending personal records.

Practical checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information or engaging a provider.
  • Document reports and follow-up reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
