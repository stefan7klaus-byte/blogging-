How Transaction Timestamps Should Be Interpreted

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Understand the issue

Explorer block timestamps are not necessarily the moment a person clicked send; wallets and exchanges may record different events.

Preserve useful records

Keep each timestamp with its source and time zone; distinguish message, withdrawal request, broadcast and block-recording times.

Interpret responsibly

Do not force different event times into a single precise sequence; document uncertainty.

Practical checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and original records.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Protect account credentials and personal information.
  • Use official reporting and support channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
