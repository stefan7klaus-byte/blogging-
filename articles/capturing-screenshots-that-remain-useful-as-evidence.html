Capturing Screenshots That Remain Useful as Evidence

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Screenshots can preserve transient pages or messages, but cropped images may omit context and metadata.

Practical steps

Capture the relevant view, include URL and date where possible, preserve the original file and note the app used.

Limitations and cautions

Screenshots can be altered; corroborate with exports, transaction records or provider statements when available.

Responsible checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
