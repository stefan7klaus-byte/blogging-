When an Exchange Record and Explorer Data Disagree

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Differences may arise from batching, fee treatment, network selection, timing or a mistaken reference.

Recommended workflow

Preserve both records, list the exact fields that differ and ask the provider for clarification using the transaction or case reference.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not silently alter amounts or select whichever source better fits a theory.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record the network, dates and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
