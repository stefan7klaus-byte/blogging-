Blockchain networks and tracing limitations

Why Exchange Internal Transfers May Not Appear On-Chain

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Understand the network

Transfers between customers at one custodial exchange may be recorded internally rather than as separate blockchain transactions.

Record relevant details

Record exchange activity entries, timestamps, asset, amount, reference number and related deposit or withdrawal hash.

Recognize the limits

A missing public transaction does not prove no internal transfer occurred; provider records may be needed.

Checklist

  • Confirm the network and exact identifier.
  • Keep source URLs and dates checked.
  • Separate direct observations from analysis.
  • Document uncertainty and alternatives.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
