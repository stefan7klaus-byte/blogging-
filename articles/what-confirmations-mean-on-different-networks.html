What Confirmations Mean on Different Networks

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Understand the issue

Confirmation conventions and finality differ by network; some interfaces use terms such as finalized or safe.

Preserve useful records

Record network, block, exact status wording, confirmation count, explorer URL and time checked.

Interpret responsibly

A confirmation count is not a universal guarantee; explain the network and source.

Practical checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and original records.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Protect account credentials and personal information.
  • Use official reporting and support channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
