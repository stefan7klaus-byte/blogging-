Transaction Hashes: A Practical Verification Guide

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Start with verifiable information

A transaction hash, sometimes called a transaction ID, is a unique reference for locating a transaction on a particular blockchain. Copy it carefully and check it on an explorer appropriate to that network.

2. Document the process

Record the network, hash, status, block or confirmation information, timestamp and relevant transfer details. Keep the explorer URL so another reviewer can repeat the check.

3. Understand the limitations

A hash helps locate a ledger record; it does not by itself identify the person who initiated the transfer or prove the surrounding story. Preserve related communications and receipts as separate evidence.

Practical checklist

  • Record dates and time zones consistently.
  • Keep transaction hashes, addresses, receipts and messages together.
  • Save source links and preserve original records.
  • Use verified official reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
