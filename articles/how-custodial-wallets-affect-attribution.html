Blockchain networks and tracing limitations

How Custodial Wallets Affect Attribution

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Understand the network

Custodial services may use wallet infrastructure for many customers, so an address does not necessarily identify one customer.

Record relevant details

Record address, network, source, claimed service association and date checked; note what official records would be needed.

Recognize the limits

Public labels can be incomplete or stale; do not assert customer identity from an address label alone.

Checklist

  • Confirm the network and exact identifier.
  • Keep source URLs and dates checked.
  • Separate direct observations from analysis.
  • Document uncertainty and alternatives.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
