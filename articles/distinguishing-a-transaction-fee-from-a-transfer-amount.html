Distinguishing a Transaction Fee From a Transfer Amount

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

Fee handling affects the totals in a tracing summary and can differ across networks or services.

Recommended workflow

Record gross amount, fee, net amount where supported and the source of each figure; specify the asset used to pay the fee.

Limitations and safeguards

Do not infer the amount received solely from the sender's displayed debit.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
