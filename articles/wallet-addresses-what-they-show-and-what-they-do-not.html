Wallet Addresses: What They Show and What They Do Not

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Start with verifiable information

Treat a wallet address as a network identifier, not a person's name. Check the relevant blockchain and verify the exact address before recording it in a case file.

2. Document the process

Record where the address appeared, the related transaction IDs, timestamps and any public labels. Save source links and clearly separate observed facts from hypotheses.

3. Understand the limitations

Address labels can be wrong or outdated, and multiple people or services may use shared infrastructure. Identity attribution requires corroboration beyond a public address.

Practical checklist

  • Record dates and time zones consistently.
  • Keep transaction hashes, addresses, receipts and messages together.
  • Save source links and preserve original records.
  • Use verified official reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
