What a Blockchain Tracing Report Can Contribute to Recovery

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A trace can organize public transaction facts and help identify questions for exchanges, investigators or legal counsel.

Recommended lawful workflow

Ask for reproducible sources, clear transaction links, assumptions, limitations and a distinction between observed facts and inferences.

Limitations and safeguards

A report is evidence support, not a recovery guarantee or proof of real-world identity.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and transaction hashes.
  • Record the network, amount, timestamps and time zone.
  • Separate verified facts from assumptions.
  • Use official exchange, law-enforcement and legal channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
