Recognizing the Advance-Fee Recovery Pattern

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A common second-stage fraud asks a victim to pay a new fee before supposed recovered assets can be released.

Recommended workflow

Preserve the exact promise, fee demand, payment destination and sender identity claims; compare them against official provider information.

Limitations and safeguards

A fee request is not proof of fraud by itself, but guaranteed returns and escalating charges are serious warning signs.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
