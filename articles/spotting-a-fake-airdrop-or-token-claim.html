Spotting a Fake Airdrop or Token Claim

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Understand the warning signs

Fake airdrop pages may use copied branding, misleading token names or wallet-connection prompts to induce unsafe signatures or reveal sensitive information.

2. Preserve relevant records

Record the page URL, domain, token contract if relevant, message history and wallet prompt details. If you connected a wallet, review permissions using a trusted workflow.

3. Take safer next steps

Do not enter a seed phrase on a claim site or sign a transaction you do not understand. A token name or logo alone does not prove legitimacy.

Practical checklist

  • Keep source URLs, dates and relevant records.
  • Separate observable facts from assumptions.
  • Protect account credentials and personal information.
  • Use official channels and independently verify providers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
