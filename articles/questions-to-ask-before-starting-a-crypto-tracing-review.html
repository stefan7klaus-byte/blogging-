Questions to Ask Before Starting a Crypto Tracing Review

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A short intake conversation can clarify whether the available evidence supports a useful review.

Recommended workflow

Ask what questions the work will answer, what sources will be examined, what is excluded, what it costs and how results will be reported.

Limitations and safeguards

Tracing may document movements without identifying a recipient or enabling recovery.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
