How to Choose a Qualified Blockchain Analysis Professional

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Overview

A credible professional should explain methods, limits, fees, privacy practices and deliverables before work begins.

Recommended workflow

Ask for written scope, sample redacted report, relevant experience, conflict-of-interest policy and secure evidence handling.

Limitations and safeguards

Be cautious of guaranteed recovery, unverifiable credentials or requests for seed phrases.

Practical checklist

  • Preserve original records and source URLs.
  • Record network, date and time zone where relevant.
  • Separate direct observations from assumptions.
  • Use verified official support and reporting channels.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
