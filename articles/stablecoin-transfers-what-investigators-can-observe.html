Blockchain networks and tracing limitations

Stablecoin Transfers: What Investigators Can Observe

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

1. Identify the relevant network data

Stablecoins are tokens issued under particular contracts and may operate on more than one blockchain. Identify the chain and token contract before interpreting a transfer or comparing amounts.

2. Preserve the transaction details

Record the transaction hash, network, token contract, amount, sender and recipient, plus any relevant provider or exchange case number. Check official token and network information when confirming which asset was involved.

3. State the limitations honestly

Stablecoin issuers and platforms may have specific controls or policies, but a public transfer does not guarantee that an issuer can or will reverse it. Do not promise freezes or recovery based solely on a trace.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm the network and asset type.
  • Record hashes, timestamps and source links.
  • Separate public ledger facts from attribution hypotheses.
  • Document gaps and uncertainty.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
