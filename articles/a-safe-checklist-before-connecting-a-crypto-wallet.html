A Safe Checklist Before Connecting a Crypto Wallet

Zenith Hackers Intelligence · Educational guide

Practical first steps

Malicious sites can request approvals or signatures that give them permission to move assets or execute actions.

Verify and preserve

Verify the domain, understand the requested permissions, review transaction details and consider using a separate low-value wallet for testing.

Protect yourself

A signature may authorize more than a simple login; never approve prompts you do not understand.

Checklist

  • Use independently verified websites and contact channels.
  • Keep relevant records secure and originals unchanged.
  • Review privacy and fees before sharing information.
  • Document reports and reference numbers.
  • Never share seed phrases, private keys, passwords or one-time codes.
